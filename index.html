How this tracker works

Stool changes are one of the earliest, easiest signs of how your dog is doing. This tool helps you record them consistently so you and your veterinarian can see patterns over time.

It compares your dog to your dog

Healthy stool looks different on different diets. Foods with more fiber tend to produce more stool; lower-residue foods tend to produce less. Neither is "better" on that basis. After your first several logs, the tracker learns your dog's usual range and watches for changes from it.

The score

Scores run from 1 (hard pellets) to 7 (liquid). Many dogs' normal falls around 2–3, but your dog's own steady pattern matters more than any single number. Stool that is too hard is also worth noticing.

The photo suggestion

When you add a photo, an AI model suggests a score and points out anything visible, like mucus or blood. It is an estimate, not a measurement, and it has not been validated against veterinary scoring. You always choose the final score. The app records whether you agreed with the suggestion.

What this tool cannot do

It cannot detect parasite eggs, hidden blood, infections, or the cause of any change. Only a veterinarian, often with a lab test, can do that. It does not diagnose, and it does not recommend or rate any food.
